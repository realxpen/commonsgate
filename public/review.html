Can people understand and challenge the rules?

This worksheet helps a facilitator discuss the existing allow / community veto / withdrawal demonstrations. It does not validate community needs by itself, and no real clinical information is involved.

Before you begin

Participation should be voluntary. Do not enter names, contact details, health experiences, or any personal information. This form only accepts predefined choices. Nothing is transmitted, collected by the server, or stored in this browser. Export creates a JSON file on your computer; keep that file private and share only appropriate summaries.

This is an exploratory review aid, not approved human-subject research, clinical evaluation, or evidence of SRHIN endorsement.

1. Watch the three scenarios

  1. Allow: check that both community reviews, individual permissions, policy, and steward check pass.
  2. Deny: observe one reviewer's veto blocking access despite other approvals.
  3. Revoke: observe an access token working first, then failing after a fictional participant withdraws permission.

2. Complete an optional review

Choose one answer per question. These are comprehension and usability signals, not evidence that a community has endorsed a governance model.

3. Facilitate the discussion

Ask what is missing from the simulated rules, who should be able to appeal, and whose interests might not be represented. Do not assume this prototype defines the right governance model. See the evaluation protocol for a safe, exploratory review approach.
