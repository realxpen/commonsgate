04 / INTENT & BOUNDARIES

Built to test an idea.
Not to make decisions for people.

CommonsGate explores one question: can collective decisions and individual rights actually control technical access, instead of living only in policy documents?

✓Inside the demoInvented participants, scripted review decisions, scoped tokens, fictional aggregates, reason-coded receipts.
×Outside the demoReal health records, patients, clinical advice, legal approval, live payments, SRHIN integration, real authentication.
